Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about investing, balances, and rewards.

How does stock pricing work?
Share prices are updated by the platform admin at midnight each day. When you buy, you buy at the current displayed price. When you convert, you receive the current price at that moment.
What is the lock-in period?
The lock-in period is based on the number of shares you buy. If you buy 5 shares, that position is locked for 5 days. If you later buy 3 more shares of the same stock using the same balance source, the lock-in becomes additive.
How does the 5% daily income work?
You earn 5% daily based on your initial invested amount. This is credited to your Main Balance at midnight every day as long as you hold active positions.
What is Frozen Balance?
Frozen Balance is a special balance credited by admin, usually for promoters. It can be used to buy stocks, but when those stocks are converted, the payout returns to Frozen Balance instead of Main Balance.
Can I withdraw Frozen Balance?
No. Frozen Balance is designed for investment activity only. It cannot be withdrawn directly.
How does the referral bonus work?
When someone registers using your referral code and makes an approved deposit, you receive 10% of that deposit amount as cashback in your Main Balance.
Can I mix Main Balance and Frozen Balance in one purchase?
No. Each stock purchase must use either Main Balance or Frozen Balance. This keeps the conversion payout routing clean and accurate.
