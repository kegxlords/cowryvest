About Cowryvest

Cowryvest is a modern investment platform designed to give users a simple way to invest in company shares, earn daily income on active positions, and grow through referrals. Our goal is to make investing accessible, transparent, and rewarding.

Share Investing

Buy shares at the current market price and convert them later based on the updated value. Your profit or loss depends on market movement at the time of conversion.

Daily Income

Active investments earn a 5% daily return based on the initial invested amount. Rewards are credited automatically at midnight.

Promoter System

Frozen Balance allows administrators to credit promoters with investment capital that can be used within the platform ecosystem.

Referral Rewards

Invite friends using your referral link. When they make an approved deposit, you earn 10% cashback in your Main Balance.

Secure Architecture

Built with Supabase authentication, Vercel serverless APIs, and role-based admin controls to keep user data and platform operations secure.

Transparent History

Every deposit, withdrawal, purchase, conversion, bonus, and yield credit is recorded in your transaction history.

Our Mission

Cowryvest exists to simplify investing for everyday users. We combine share-based investment opportunities, daily rewards, and referral incentives into one clean platform. Whether you are a regular investor or a promoter, Cowryvest gives you a structured way to participate and grow.
